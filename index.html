Introduction

About Me

I'm Visalakshi, an AI Developer based in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, specializing in machine learning, generative AI, and agentic systems. I build applications that turn raw data and natural language into practical, reliable tools — from LLM-powered analysis pipelines to RAG systems and AI agents.

My technical foundation spans Python, applied machine learning, and local LLM development using LangChain and Ollama, alongside agentic AI workflows and the Claude API. I'm also comfortable across the web stack — HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and PHP — which lets me ship complete, usable interfaces around the systems I build, not just the models underneath. I build systems that are useful, explainable, and privacy-first by design.

Alongside AI work, I build in Unity — most recently an AR/VR learning platform for children with autism — because I'm just as interested in who technology reaches as in how it works.
